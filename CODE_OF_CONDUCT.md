# BibzCode Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free, and technically constructive
community for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, disability,
appearance, nationality, ethnicity, religion, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Community members are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and assume good intent while evaluating claims on evidence;
- provide actionable technical feedback and accept correction gracefully;
- respect privacy, consent, licensing, and responsible-disclosure boundaries;
- keep issues and pull requests focused on the project;
- credit others' work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- help make participation safe for newcomers as well as experienced contributors.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, insults, sexualized content, or unwanted
  attention;
- doxxing or publishing private information without explicit permission;
- deliberately exposing credentials, vulnerabilities, or personal user data;
- spam, impersonation, plagiarism, malicious code, hidden telemetry, or social
  engineering;
- sustained disruption, bad-faith argument, or retaliation against a reporter;
- using project spaces to coordinate illegal or harmful activity.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
release channels, and other spaces where someone represents BibzCode or its community.

## Reporting

Do not post sensitive evidence publicly. Contact the repository maintainer privately
using the contact options on the
[`XbibzOfficial777` GitHub profile](https://github.com/XbibzOfficial777). If the
behavior occurs on GitHub, you may also use GitHub's block and report-abuse tools.
Security vulnerabilities must follow `SECURITY.md` instead.

Include the location, date, people involved, description, and any privately shared
evidence. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Knowingly false or
retaliatory reports may themselves violate this policy.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict
participation, reject contributions, or permanently ban a participant. Enforcement
will consider severity, intent, repeated behavior, impact, and willingness to repair
harm. Maintainers who are directly involved in a report should recuse themselves when
another suitable reviewer is available.

Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report is prohibited.
